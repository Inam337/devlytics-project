About us

Built by engineers who distrust vanity metrics

Devlytics started inside an engineering organization that was tired of measuring lines of code. We wanted a system where a high ranking means the code is genuinely better — tested, maintainable, reviewed — and where a low score comes with a concrete plan instead of a lecture.

Analysis runs on your own infrastructure by default. Static analysis produces the evidence, AI interprets it, and the platform proves whether an improvement actually moved the metric.
